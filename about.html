About Us

Building Strength.
Building Trust.

GAGI SACCO is a manufacturer and supplier of high quality precast concrete products. We are committed to providing durable, reliable and affordable solutions for construction and infrastructure development.

Who We Are

A Local SACCO with a Strong Vision

GAGI SACCO was established to meet the growing demand for quality precast concrete products in Murang'a County and beyond. Through teamwork, innovation and dedication, we continue to support our community by delivering products that build stronger structures and better futures.
